## The supplies

Alongside the peptide itself: bacteriostatic water if it's sold separately, insulin syringes in the right size, alcohol swabs, and a sharps disposal container. Having all of these ready before the first dose arrives avoids a delay once you're set to start.

### Where these commonly come from in the UK

Syringes and alcohol swabs are widely available from UK pharmacies without prescription. Sharps bins can be sourced from a pharmacy or ordered online. Some peptide suppliers bundle some of these items with an order; checking what's included before buying duplicates is worth doing.

## The tracking setup

A notebook, spreadsheet, or app, whichever format you'll actually use consistently, set up with the basic fields (date, dose, site, weight) before the first entry rather than figuring out the format after you've already started.

### A scale worth trusting

If tracking weight weekly, a scale that gives consistent readings matters more than one with elaborate extra features. Placing it on a hard, level surface and weighing at a consistent time gives more reliable week-to-week comparisons than an inconsistent routine with a fancier scale.

## A short pre-start checklist

Peptide and reconstitution supplies sourced and confirmed. Injection supplies and sharps disposal ready. A tracking format chosen and set up. A rough idea of your dosing schedule for the first few weeks.
