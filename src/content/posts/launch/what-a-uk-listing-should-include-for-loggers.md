## The mg content, COA-confirmed

A listing should clearly state the vial's mg content, backed by a batch-specific certificate of analysis. This is the single most important number for every calculation a log will eventually depend on.

### Why this matters more for a first order

A first-time buyer starting a log has nothing to compare a new listing against yet. A clearly documented, COA-backed mg figure removes one major source of uncertainty right at the start, before any log entries exist to cross-check against.

## Reconstitution guidance, if available

A listing that includes a suggested water volume and resulting concentration, clearly separated from the COA-verified mg content, makes the first few log entries considerably easier to set up correctly.

### Dispatch and delivery detail

Same-day dispatch cutoffs and realistic UK delivery estimates help plan exactly when a first dose, and the first log entry, will actually happen, rather than leaving that date uncertain.

## Supplies included or not

A listing that's explicit about whether syringes, swabs or bacteriostatic water are included avoids a first-time buyer being caught short on injection day without realising a separate order was needed.

## The overall picture

A listing with COA-backed content, clear reconstitution guidance, realistic delivery information, and clarity on what's included gives a first-time logger everything needed to start a log accurately from week one.
