## The baseline number

A 12-week retatrutide log with one injection per week needs a minimum of 12 syringes. In practice, buying more than the bare minimum is worth doing, since a bent needle, a dropped syringe, or a second draw needed after a reconstitution adjustment can use up a spare without warning. Fifteen to twenty syringes is a more realistic number to buy for a smooth 12-week course.

### Why spares matter more than they seem

A syringe used incorrectly, contaminated by touching the needle, or bent while drawing from a vial, needs to be discarded and replaced rather than reused. Without spares on hand, this turns into an unplanned trip to the pharmacy on injection day, which is avoidable by simply buying a few extra from the start.

## Where to buy them in the UK

UK pharmacies sell packs of 10 over the counter, no prescription needed, for around £5 to £10. Online medical suppliers sell boxes of 100 at a lower per-unit price, which covers several 12-week courses in one order and removes the need to reorder partway through. For a single 12-week log, a box of 100 is more than enough and works out considerably cheaper than repeated pharmacy visits.

### Choosing the right size

Retatrutide doses are typically small volumes, so a 0.5ml syringe with a 29 to 31 gauge needle gives a clearer reading than a 1ml syringe for most weekly doses. Confirming the syringe size matches the expected dose volume before buying in bulk avoids ending up with the wrong size for the whole course.

## Matching supply to the log

Buying syringes in a single batch at the start of a 12-week course means every week uses the same size and gauge, which keeps entries in the log consistent from week one to week twelve. If a size does need to change partway through, noting that change in the log explains any difference in how a dose reads on paper.

### Counting out supplies weekly

Some loggers count out a week's worth of syringes into a small container at the start of each week, alongside the rest of that week's supplies. This makes it obvious at a glance whether the stock is running low well before it actually runs out.

## What some retatrutide listings include

A handful of UK retatrutide sellers bundle a starter supply of syringes with the first order. Checking the listing's included-items section before buying a separate box for a full 12-week course avoids ending up with more syringes than needed in the first few weeks.

## What to do with leftover syringes

If a course ends with syringes still remaining, whether because a bulk box was bought or the course finished earlier than planned, they don't need to go to waste. Unopened, individually wrapped syringes generally keep for years if stored in a dry place, so a leftover stock is simply a head start on the next course, if one's planned, or something to check the use-by date on periodically if not.

### Disposing of unused syringes safely

An unused syringe still needs safe disposal if it's no longer needed at all, though it doesn't need to go through the sharps process the same way a used one does, since there's no risk of contamination. Most pharmacies will still take unopened stock back if asked, or it can be kept as a general first-aid supply.

### Storing syringes properly while unused

Keeping unopened syringes in their original packaging, in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, preserves their sterility and integrity for as long as the manufacturer's stated shelf life, generally several years. A bathroom cabinet exposed to steam and humidity isn't ideal for long-term storage; a bedroom drawer or a dedicated supply box is a better choice.

### A final sense-check on quantity

Counting the planned course length in weeks, adding a third again for spares, and rounding up to the nearest pack size is a quick way to land on a sensible order quantity without under-buying or significantly over-buying for a single 12-week course.

## A short buying checklist

Buy 15 to 20 syringes for a 12-week course to allow for spares, choose 0.5ml syringes with a 29 to 31 gauge needle for typical dose volumes, check what the retatrutide listing already includes before ordering separately, and keep the same size and gauge consistent across the full course to keep the log easy to compare week to week.
