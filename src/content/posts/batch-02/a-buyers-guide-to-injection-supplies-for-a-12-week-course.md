## Syringes: buy more than the bare minimum

A 12-week course needs 12 syringes at minimum, but 15 to 20 is a more realistic number once spares for a bent needle or a second draw are accounted for. UK pharmacies sell packs of 10 for £5 to £10; online medical suppliers sell boxes of 100 for a better per-unit price. A 0.5ml syringe with a 29 to 31 gauge needle suits most early doses.

### Matching size to the format

A pen device needs no separate syringe for injecting, only for drawing if reconstituting a vial-fed cartridge. A vial-and-syringe setup needs the syringe for both drawing and injecting, so confirming which format is being used before buying avoids purchasing the wrong supplies.

## Alcohol swabs: cheap and easy to over-buy slightly

A box of 100 individually wrapped 70% isopropyl alcohol swabs, priced £2 to £5 from any UK pharmacy, comfortably covers a 12-week course with plenty left over. There's no real downside to buying more than needed here, since they don't expire quickly and cost very little.

### Where to buy them

Pharmacy shelves, supermarkets with a pharmacy counter, and online medical suppliers all stock them without a prescription.

## Sharps disposal: sort this before day one

A UN3291-rated sharps bin, sized 1 litre for a single person's weekly course, costs £5 to £15 from a pharmacy or online supplier. This should be bought and ready before the first injection, not ordered afterwards once a used needle already needs somewhere to go.

### Council exchange schemes

Many UK councils offer a free sharps bin exchange, worth checking before buying a second bin later in the course.

## Reconstitution supplies, if needed

Bacteriostatic water, if not included with the peptide order, is sold by the same suppliers and pharmacies stocking the other supplies. Confirming the water volume needed against the listing's reconstitution guidance before ordering avoids buying the wrong quantity.

## Optional extras worth considering

A body diagram for tracking injection site rotation, a numbing cream if injection comfort is a concern, and a travel case if any part of the course involves being away from home. None of these are essential, but each solves a specific problem that comes up for some people during a 12-week course.

## A total rough budget for the course

Adding up syringes (£10 to £20 for enough plus spares), swabs (£2 to £5), a sharps bin (£5 to £15), and bacteriostatic water if needed (£5 to £10), the supporting supplies for a full 12-week course typically come to somewhere between £25 and £50 in the UK, separate from the cost of the peptide itself. This is a one-off setup cost weighted toward the first week, not a recurring weekly expense.

### Where a bundle changes this math

A starter bundle from a peptide seller that includes several of these items can bring the total down slightly and simplify ordering into a single delivery, though it's worth checking the bundle's exact contents against this list to confirm nothing essential is missing before assuming it's complete.

## Ordering timeline for a smooth start

Placing the main supply order seven to ten days before the planned start date, rather than right before, allows for normal UK delivery variation without risking a delayed first dose. A shorter secondary order, for anything discovered to be missing once the first delivery arrives, can follow with a few days' buffer before it's actually needed.

### Keeping receipts and order confirmations

Saving order confirmations and delivery receipts for the course's supplies, even just as emails filed in a folder, is a simple habit that helps if a reorder is needed later or if a specific batch or product needs referencing again months into a course.

### A final sense-check before ordering

Reading back through the full list once, peptide, syringes, swabs, sharps bin, water if needed, against the actual planned course length catches most quantity mistakes before an order is placed rather than after supplies run short in week eight.

## A short shopping list

15 to 20 syringes (0.5ml, 29 to 31 gauge). A box of alcohol swabs. A 1 litre UN3291-rated sharps bin. Bacteriostatic water, if needed, matched to the listing's guidance. A tracking format set up before day one. Optional: a rotation chart, numbing cream, a travel case. Sourcing all of this before the course starts, rather than piecemeal, keeps the first weeks of a log complete from the start.
